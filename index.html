How it works

At a roadside inspection the conversation with the officer is in English. The officer asks where you are coming from and where you are going, what you are hauling, when you started your shift, and asks you to show your license, medical card and logs. Many experienced drivers know the job perfectly well but freeze when the questions come fast, with an accent they are not used to, at the side of a busy road.

Inspection English is a short daily practice built only around this situation. It is not a general English course with grammar tables and travel phrases. Every lesson is a small piece of a real inspection: a question spoken at normal speed, a pause for your answer, and a model answer you can repeat. You speak out loud into the phone; the app compares your answer with the expected one and marks the words that were missing or unclear.

The lessons follow the order of an inspection. First the greeting and the reason for the stop. Then questions about the trip: origin, destination, the company you drive for, the load and its weight. Then documents: how to name each paper you carry and where it is in the cab. Then hours of service: when you started, when you took your break, how to read your logs out loud. Finally road signs and short instructions — the words you need to describe a sign or to repeat what the officer told you.

Every phrase you miss goes to your review list. The next day the drill starts with those phrases, so ten minutes a day are spent on what you really need, not on what you already know. You can choose a slower voice at the beginning and move to normal speed when you are ready. Lessons work between loads, at a truck stop or during a break; you need only your phone and headphones.

The practice is written for drivers whose first language is not English: the instructions are short and clear, and each model answer is simple, polite and true to the situation. We do not promise any result at an inspection — the officer decides — but a driver who has answered the same questions out loud fifty times is calmer when they come for real.

You start by choosing your level and the kind of freight you haul. The first lessons check what you already say well, and the plan is built from what is left. After a few weeks the drill becomes a quick daily repetition, and you can always go back to any part of the inspection.

Questions

Is this a full English course?

No. It covers only the conversation at a roadside inspection: questions, answers about your trip, documents, hours and road signs.

Do I need to type?

No. You listen and answer out loud; the app checks your spoken answer and shows the words you missed.

Can I practice slowly first?

Yes. You can start with a slower voice and switch to normal speed when you are ready.

Do you guarantee I will pass an inspection?

No. The officer decides. The practice helps you understand the questions and answer them clearly.

Can I use it already?

Not yet. The service is in preparation, and we are checking whether drivers need it.
